## Highlights

- **Terminal, App and Files on a stopped VM.** In PVM Desktop they stay on when the VM is stopped, on the VMs screen and for a workspace whose VM is stopped.
  - Terminal and Files ask first, naming the VM and what opens once it is ready. App says so in its own dialog, whose button becomes "Start and open", so there is one question only.
  - The VM is started with its stages shown in its row, and the terminal, the app or the file manager opens once it is ready. `pvm ssh` no longer asks in the console.
  - On a VM that is starting, App and Files wait for it instead of failing.
  - A failed start, or a stop during the wait, opens nothing, and the command is left in Activity to run by hand.
- **App settings in groups.** The seven settings stand in three groups, Interaction, Performance and Security, in two columns, so all show at once down to the smallest window.
  - Each setting says when a change applies, and the long explanations open with a button.
  - A changed setting is marked with what it was, and Apply counts the changes.
  - Leaving the kept keyring password warns, as it is chosen, that apps sign in again once the VM restarts.
  - The create form uses the same groups and controls.

## Upgrading

- **Use the Windows installer** to get the new PVM Desktop. `pvm upgrade` still replaces `pvm.exe` only, and nothing in `pvm.exe` changed.

## Known issues

- `pvm app stop <vm> <id>` can report `exit status 1` with no message although the app stopped, and an app started right after can fail while its unit is still stopping. The cause has not been looked into.
- In Chromium and Electron apps, a text cursor that moves without a click, a focus change or committed text is not reported, so the candidates stay where they were.
- On macOS and Linux hosts the candidates still open at the top left of the window, and the microphone does not follow a new default device.
- WHPX VMs still have no AVX.
